Robot: Car like robot with 4 wheels.
Direction: Controlled via servo with Ackerman Mechanism attached to the 2 front wheels.
Rear wheels: 2 wheels each connected to an independent DC motor with gear reduction mechanism and controlled via MD22 H bridge.
Turning Radius: 50 cm.
Maximum speed of the robot 20cm/second when motors are at 90% of their power.
Minumun power sent to motors to make the robot move: 50% of their power at 8cm/second.
Distance between front and back wheels: 34cm
Maximum range for servo controlling the front direction mechanism: 45 to 135 degrees.
Central position for servo controlling the front direction mechanism: 90 degrees
