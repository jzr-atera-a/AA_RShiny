title: Batch ML Pipeline
variant: Scheduled training and bulk scoring (MLOps)
summary: A scheduled pipeline ingests and validates data, builds features, trains and evaluates a model, registers it behind an approval gate and then scores whole datasets in bulk. Results land in a store that reports and applications read, while a monitor watches data and model drift to trigger retraining.
when_to_use: Predictions are needed daily or weekly for many records at once, such as churn scores, demand forecasts or lead scoring.
when_not_to_use: Decisions must be made per request in real time.
complexity: Medium
tags: batch, mlops, training, scoring, orchestration, drift

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Simple, cheap and easy to debug compared with real-time serving.

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Heavy features and large models are fine because latency is not critical.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Predictions are only as fresh as the last run.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Not suitable for decisions that depend on events within the last minutes.

@tradeoff
kind: risk
text: A silent upstream schema change can corrupt a whole batch; validation gates are essential.

@tradeoff
kind: cost
text: Pay only while jobs run, but backfills can be expensive.

@tradeoff
kind: scaling
text: Partition data and scale the scoring job out; keep runs idempotent so they can be retried.
