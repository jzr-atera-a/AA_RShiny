design_id: seed_rag
design_group: ml_genai
design_type: rag
title: Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
variant: Hybrid search with reranking and citations
summary: RAG grounds a large language model in private or fresh knowledge. Documents are chunked, embedded and indexed offline. At question time the system retrieves the most relevant chunks, adds them to the prompt and the model answers from them with citations, so knowledge changes without retraining.
when_to_use: Answers must be based on private, frequently changing or auditable documents, such as policies, manuals, tickets or contracts.
when_not_to_use: The task needs no external knowledge, or the whole corpus fits comfortably in the model context window.
complexity: Medium
tags: rag, llm, vector search, embeddings, grounding

@step
order: 1
text: Source documents are parsed and split into chunks with metadata and permissions.
components: C13,C14

@step
order: 2
text: Chunks are embedded and written to the vector, keyword and document stores.
components: C14,C15,C7,C8,C9

@step
order: 3
text: The user asks a question; the API authenticates, checks the semantic cache and passes the query on.
components: C1,C2,C16

@step
order: 4
text: The query is rewritten and embedded.
components: C3,C4

@step
order: 5
text: Hybrid retrieval searches both indexes and merges candidates.
components: C5,C7,C8

@step
order: 6
text: The reranker keeps the best chunks and their full passages are fetched.
components: C6,C9

@step
order: 7
text: The context builder assembles the prompt, the LLM answers and guardrails verify the citations.
components: C10,C11,C12

@step
order: 8
text: The cited answer returns to the user and feedback is logged for tuning.
components: C2,C1,C17

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Answers are grounded in your data and can cite their sources.

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Knowledge is updated by re-indexing, with no model retraining.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Answer quality is capped by retrieval quality, so chunking and ranking need ongoing tuning.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Retrieval and reranking add latency to every question.

@tradeoff
kind: risk
text: Stale, duplicated or poisoned documents lead to confident wrong answers.

@tradeoff
kind: cost
text: Embedding, vector storage and reranking add recurring cost on top of LLM tokens.

@tradeoff
kind: scaling
text: Vector index memory and ingestion throughput grow with corpus size; shard and filter by metadata.
