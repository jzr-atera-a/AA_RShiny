design_group: data_processing
design_type: kappa_architecture
title: Kappa Architecture
variant: Single streaming path with replayable log
summary: Kappa architecture removes the batch layer. All data flows through one replayable log and one stream processing codebase. To fix logic or rebuild a view, the job is redeployed and replays the log from the beginning into a new output table, which then replaces the old one.
when_to_use: Most workloads are event based, the log can retain history and one streaming engine can handle both live and reprocessed data.
when_not_to_use: Computations need huge historical scans that are not practical to replay, or ad hoc analytics over data not held in the log.
complexity: Medium
tags: kappa, streaming, event log, replay, kafka

@step
order: 1
text: Producers write events to the immutable log, validated by the schema registry.
components: C1,C3,C2

@step
order: 2
text: Job v1 processes the live stream and maintains the output table.
components: C4,C6,C7

@step
order: 3
text: To change logic, job v2 is deployed and replays the log into a new table.
components: C5,C8

@step
order: 4
text: The monitor tracks catch-up progress until lag reaches zero.
components: C11

@step
order: 5
text: The query service switches to the new table and v1 is retired.
components: C9,C8,C7

@step
order: 6
text: Applications read fresh results.
components: C9,C10

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: One codebase and one pipeline to build, test and operate.

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Reprocessing is just a replay, which makes fixes and new views simple.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Long retention of the log can be expensive.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Replaying large histories takes time and capacity.

@tradeoff
kind: risk
text: Exactly-once semantics and schema changes need careful design.

@tradeoff
kind: cost
text: High log retention and parallel jobs during reprocessing.

@tradeoff
kind: scaling
text: Scale by partitioning the log and parallelising the job per partition.
