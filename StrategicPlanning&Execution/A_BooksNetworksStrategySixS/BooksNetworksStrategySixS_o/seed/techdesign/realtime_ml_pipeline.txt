title: Real-Time ML Pipeline with Feature Store
variant: Online inference with training-serving consistency
summary: A production ML system that serves predictions in milliseconds. Streaming and batch pipelines compute features into an offline and an online feature store so training and serving see identical definitions. Models are tracked in a registry, served behind a gateway, monitored for drift, and retrained automatically.
when_to_use: Predictions must be fresh and fast, for example fraud scoring, recommendations, pricing or personalisation.
when_not_to_use: Daily or weekly scoring is enough; a batch pipeline is simpler and cheaper.
complexity: High
tags: mlops, feature store, online inference, drift, retraining

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: Fresh features and fast predictions improve accuracy for time-sensitive decisions.

@tradeoff
kind: pro
text: One feature definition for training and serving prevents training-serving skew.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Many moving parts: streaming, two stores, registry and monitoring.

@tradeoff
kind: con
text: Needs strong MLOps practice and on-call ownership.

@tradeoff
kind: risk
text: Online and offline features drifting apart silently breaks predictions.

@tradeoff
kind: cost
text: Always-on streaming and low-latency stores cost more than batch jobs.

@tradeoff
kind: scaling
text: Scale the online store and model servers horizontally; keep p99 latency budgets explicit.
