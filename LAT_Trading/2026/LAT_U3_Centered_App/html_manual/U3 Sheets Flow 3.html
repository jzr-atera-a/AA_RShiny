Set it up

1 · Install

Unzip into a new folder

  • Unzip trading_analysis_lat_2026_u3_sheets.zip into an empty folder (not over the old app).
  • Install LibreOffice, which makes the PDFs.
  • In RStudio: shiny::runApp("that folder"). The header must read LAT Unit 3 Records.
2 · Keys

Put the keys in .Renviron

  • ANTHROPIC_API_KEY: Claude reads the screenshots.
  • ATERA_BQ_KEY_FILE: path to the BigQuery JSON key.
  • No Dropbox key is needed on your PC: the app writes into your synced Dropbox folder.
  • Or paste the keys under API Configuration each session.
3 · Setup tab

Trading Records > 1. Connection & Setup

  • Create / verify tables
  • Name, LAT ID, period 12/10 to 06/11, opening balance, risk % per trade, combined cap, minimum RRR and ADX numbers. The Decision Panel checks your plan against these.
  • Period start 19/10/2026 (the end moves to Fri 13/11 by itself), opening balance 10000, Expected trade duration (hours) 10.
  • Save settings
4 · Check Dropbox

Test, then catch up

  • Test Dropbox (write a small file and read it back) should show ✓.
  • Complete past Morning Sheets and trades: every trading day since 19 Oct gets its levels. Sheets already in Dropbox are kept.
5 · First live check

Prove each link works once

  • BigQuery: the bar at the top of Trading Records is green.
  • Claude: API Configuration > Claude API > Test Connection (or set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in .Renviron so it connects at start). Without it, news, missing calendar days and actuals are skipped.
  • Yahoo: Fill Low / High from Yahoo fills Brent.
  • Dropbox: the test above shows ✓.
  • Anything red: copy the message from API Configuration > Debug Console.
Every trading day

Two lanes, both ending in Dropbox

The Morning Sheet is done once before you trade. The trade lane repeats for each trade. Every Save rebuilds the Word file and the PDF and puts them in the right folder.

Morning Sheet

about 06:30 to 07:30 UK, before any trade
  1. 2
    Prices from Yahoo check Trading Records > 2. Morning Sheet (date = today) Fill Low / High from Yahoo: every row gets its overnight low and high (21:00 UK to now), current price and time, prior high / low / close and ADX(14); Dow and Nikkei with their change. Oil is in IG points. Optional: read an IG watchlist screenshot first (Read levels with Claude) so the rows are shifted onto IG's own prices.
  2. 2
    Economic calendar: yesterday + today check Source 1. All sources: Forex Factory, then Yahoo, then Claude (default), then Load yesterday + today calendar. Fills yesterday (the previous trading day, plus the weekend on a Monday), today, and the releases inside your trading window. The notes under it say what each source gave. Other sources: Claude only, Forex Factory only, IG screenshots, FRED / EIA.
  3. 2
    News: your notes + top 3 with links you Write your own lines in Overnight news. Then choose General markets, Brent oil or Another asset (type it) and Add the top 3 news (Claude, with sources): each story gets a headline, 2-3 factual sentences of context, the source and its link.
  4. 2
    Plan for the day you Regime per instrument, set-ups you will look for, exposure, combined open risk, stand aside (and why), in your own words. Saved in BigQuery; not printed on the LAT template.
  5. SAVE
    Fill every empty cell and save app Fill every empty cell and save: anything still empty is filled (Yahoo, the calendar source, the top 3 news), then everything goes to BigQuery and the Word + PDF are built. News links are clickable on the sheet.
    BigQuery: trading_morning_sheets + trading_morning_levels; Dropbox: …\Morning Sheets\Morning_Sheet_27-10-2026.docx and .pdf
  6. 2
    Check, or reload later check Open the PDF in Dropbox. Any day can be brought back from BigQuery: Load a saved sheet > pick the date > Load. Saving again keeps the earlier version.

Individual Trade Sheet

for every trade, from decision to exit
  1. IG
    Open the deal ticket, don't place it yet you Screenshot the IG deal ticket for Brent (Sell / Buy price, size, minimum size). Crop out account names and numbers.
  2. DP
    Look at the facts check Trade Journal > Decision Panel > 1. Before the trade Upload the ticket in Or read them from an IG ticket screenshot (or type the IG price). The badge must say Levels aligned to your IG price. Read the cards: where price is, noise, regime, calendar and clock, and the levels table and chart.
  3. DP
    Decide and write your reasons you Choose Buy, Sell or No trade. Enter order type, entry, stop, target, size, chart period and expected duration. Read the risk line and any red warnings. Write the six boxes in your own words, using the facts shown under each box. For No trade, write why.
  4. SAVE
    Save decision snapshot app Save decision snapshot stores the time, your words and the facts you saw in BigQuery. It cannot be changed later without a note. Now place the trade in IG.
  5. IG
    Screenshots of the trade you Keep the ticket (its file name records when you placed the order), and after the fill or the exit take the chart (15 or 30 Mins), History, Closed in the last 24 hours and, if you can, History > Activity (the exact opening time). Crop out account names and numbers.
  6. OE
    Read them all, build one trade check Trade Journal > Order Evidence > 1. Screenshot to fields Upload all of them, Read all with Claude, glance at each item (fix a box only if it differs from the picture), then Build the trade from ALL screenshots (recommended). Direction comes from the highlighted SELL / BUY box, entry, stop and limit from the ticket or position, the chart period from the chart, the exit from History.
  7. SAVE
    Save the open trade check Trading Records > 3. Trades > A (filled in for you) Everything is filled, Assessed included. Without an Activity screenshot the fill time is estimated from Yahoo prices and ticked "Time is an estimate". Choose the strategy, then Save open trade. A decision saved earlier links itself; the chart and the ticket go on the sheet as pictures.
    Saved to BigQuery; Trade Sheet in …\Individual Trade Sheets\Individual_Trade_Sheet_Brent_27-10-2026 .docx + .pdf
  8. MG
    If you manage it you 3. Trades > B: Moved stop / Partial exit, then 5. Exports > pictures Record the time, the new stop or the size closed, and why. Save the updated chart as In-trade management, tied to that action. The LAT asks for one chart per action.
  9. OUT
    The exit is filled in next you After Save open trade the exit form (3. Trades > B) already holds the exit price, time, P/L and exit code (S1 = stop, T = target, ME = manual). Say whether you followed your plan (and if not, what happened), then Save. A trade still open: come back with the History screenshot later and use Send to Trades: exit form.
  10. 5
    Complete the sheet you 5. Exports > Individual Trade Sheet Pictures: the chart and ticket are already attached; add other timeframes or a post-exit chart if you have them, then Save picture(s) to this trade. Complete this sheet: your six boxes (from the decision, or write them here), the reason for exit and lessons, then Save my text to this trade.
  11. DONE
    Gaps panel green app Each save above rebuilt the sheet in Dropbox. A chart slot you left empty gets a chart drawn from Yahoo data, labelled as such. Replace it with your IG chart when you can.
    Final Word + PDF in Individual Trade Sheets; Trade_Blotter.xlsx updated in Jose-F_Zubizarreta
If a limit or stop order never fillsDecision Panel > 2. Decision journal: select the order, Check the order against prices, then Mark expired and save the 'order not filled' Trade Sheet. It saves Individual_Trade_Sheet_Brent_<date>_order_not_filled .docx + .pdf, with your words and a chart. Add an IG History screenshot if you have one.

Today

Daily checklist and fixes

Today's run

Ticks are kept in this browser for today only.

Decision Panel says "NOT aligned to IG"

No IG price yet. Read the ticket screenshot or type the IG Mid price in IG price now. Without it, Yahoo levels can be 150 to 250 points away from IG.

The trade did not link to my decision

It links only when market, side and day match, the decision was saved before the entry, and the entries are within 0.5%. Otherwise use Decision journal: select the decision, choose the trade under It filled: link it to this trade, then Link.

Entry time is empty

The Positions screen has no clock. Take History > Activity, read it in Order Evidence and send it: the app opens the trade in Edit trade with the real time. Press Save.

Dropbox shows ✗ or nothing was saved

Check that Dropbox is installed at C:\Users\jfz00\Dropbox and running. Setup > Test Dropbox tells you which part failed. On a server, the Dropbox app needs Full Dropbox access and a refresh token.

Word file saved but no PDF

LibreOffice is missing, or a LibreOffice window was open. Close LibreOffice (Task Manager: end soffice) and save again: any save rebuilds both.

"Claude is not connected" in the calendar or news notes

Connect it under API Configuration > Claude API > Test Connection, or put ANTHROPIC_API_KEY in .Renviron. Then load the calendar / add the news again.

"Yahoo … the page was not for that day"

Yahoo's calendar page sometimes ignores the date asked for. The app checks it against Forex Factory and drops a wrong page; Claude fills the day instead. Nothing to do.

The Debug Console

API Configuration > Debug Console shows only this app's messages since it started. Copy the red lines from there when something fails.

Low / High still empty

Yahoo was not reachable or the symbol had no bars. The Morning tab lists each problem under the Yahoo box. Try again, or read the range from an IG hourly chart and type it.

A chart says the Yahoo prices do not reach the IG exit

Yahoo's Brent future and IG's price differ that day. Replace the drawn chart with your own IG screenshot in Exports > pictures.

Wrong trade closed by a History row

Choose the right trade in 3. Trades > B before saving. Then use Edit trade on any trade, open or closed, to correct it. Every change is a new version.

The Unit 3 rule the app keepsClaude only copies what your screenshots show and finds published data with its sources. It never writes your set-up, trigger, execution, reasons, lessons, plan, evaluation or SWOT. Those must be your own words and judgement (Learner Statement).
