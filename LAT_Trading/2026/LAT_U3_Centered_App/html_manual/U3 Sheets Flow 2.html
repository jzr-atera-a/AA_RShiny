Set it up

1 · Install

Unzip into a new folder

  • Unzip trading_analysis_lat_2026_u3_sheets.zip into an empty folder (not over the old app).
  • Install LibreOffice, which makes the PDFs.
  • In RStudio: shiny::runApp("that folder"). The header must read LAT Unit 3 Records.
2 · Keys

Put the keys in .Renviron

  • ANTHROPIC_API_KEY: Claude reads the screenshots.
  • ATERA_BQ_KEY_FILE: path to the BigQuery JSON key.
  • No Dropbox key is needed on your PC: the app writes into your synced Dropbox folder.
  • Or paste the keys under API Configuration each session.
3 · Setup tab

Trading Records > 1. Connection & Setup

  • Create / verify tables
  • Name, LAT ID, period 12/10 to 06/11, opening balance, risk % per trade, combined cap, minimum RRR and ADX numbers. The Decision Panel checks your plan against these.
  • Your plan durations, e.g. 15 Min = 4; 1 Hour = 8; Daily = 48
  • Save settings
4 · Check Dropbox

Test, then catch up

  • Test Dropbox (write a small file and read it back) should show ✓.
  • Complete past Morning Sheets and trades: every trading day since 12 Oct gets its levels. Sheets already in Dropbox are kept.
5 · First live check

Prove each link works once

  • BigQuery: the bar at the top of Trading Records is green.
  • Claude: Order Evidence shows "Claude API connected"; read one watchlist screenshot.
  • Yahoo: Fill Low / High from Yahoo fills Brent.
  • Dropbox: the test above shows ✓.
  • Anything red: copy the message from API Configuration > Debug Console.
Every trading day

Two lanes, both ending in Dropbox

The Morning Sheet is done once before you trade. The trade lane repeats for each trade. Every Save rebuilds the Word file and the PDF and puts them in the right folder.

Morning Sheet

about 06:30 to 07:30 UK, before any trade
  1. IG
    Take the IG screenshots you Watchlist showing Wall Street, Japan 225, EUR/USD, USD/JPY, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, Oil - US Crude, Spot Gold and Oil - Brent Crude. Then the economic calendar for yesterday and for today. Crop out account names and numbers.
  2. 2
    Read the prices check Trading Records > 2. Morning Sheet > I will upload screenshots Upload the watchlist, then Read levels with Claude. Prices and Time now go into the rows; compare them with the picture.
  3. 2
    Read the calendar check Upload yesterday's and today's calendar screenshots, then Read calendar. The tables fill, and the pictures are kept for the sheet.
  4. 2
    Write your overnight news you A few lines in your own words in Overnight news. Leave it empty if you want Claude to add sourced headlines in the next step.
  5. SAVE
    Fill every empty cell and save app Fill every empty cell and save: Yahoo fills the overnight Low and High, any missing price, the time, and the Dow and Nikkei change. Claude fills an empty news box or a missing calendar, with links. Your IG values are kept.
    Saved to BigQuery and to Dropbox\London Academy of Trading\Jose-F_Zubizarreta\Morning Sheets\Morning_Sheet_26-10-2026.docx and .pdf
  6. 5
    Look at the gaps panel check 5. Exports > Morning Sheet Green ✓ means every box is filled. Anything listed in red: fix it on the Morning tab and save again. The earlier file goes to _previous versions.

Individual Trade Sheet

for every trade, from decision to exit
  1. IG
    Open the deal ticket, don't place it yet you Screenshot the IG deal ticket for Brent (Sell / Buy price, size, minimum size). Crop out account names and numbers.
  2. DP
    Look at the facts check Trade Journal > Decision Panel > 1. Before the trade Upload the ticket in Or read them from an IG ticket screenshot (or type the IG price). The badge must say Levels aligned to your IG price. Read the cards: where price is, noise, regime, calendar and clock, and the levels table and chart.
  3. DP
    Decide and write your reasons you Choose Buy, Sell or No trade. Enter order type, entry, stop, target, size, chart period and expected duration. Read the risk line and any red warnings. Write the six boxes in your own words, using the facts shown under each box. For No trade, write why.
  4. SAVE
    Save decision snapshot app Save decision snapshot stores the time, your words and the facts you saw in BigQuery. It cannot be changed later without a note. Now place the trade in IG.
  5. IG
    After the fill: two screenshots you The Positions row (entry, stop, limit) and History > Activity, which shows the time the position opened.
  6. OE
    Read them with Claude check Trade Journal > Order Evidence > 1. Screenshot to fields Source: Desktop website or Mobile app. Upload both, then Read all with Claude. Pick each item, check the values against the picture, then Send to Trades (right form for this screen).
  7. SAVE
    Save the open trade check Trading Records > 3. Trades > A The form is filled from the screenshots. Choose Assessed and the strategy, then Save open trade. The app finds your decision (same market, side and day, entry close by) and links it: your six boxes and chart period fill the trade, and your ticket screenshot moves to its sheet.
    Saved to BigQuery; first Trade Sheet in …\Individual Trade Sheets\Individual_Trade_Sheet_Brent_26-10-2026 .docx + .pdf
  8. MG
    If you manage it you 3. Trades > B: Moved stop / Partial exit, then 5. Exports > pictures Record the time, the new stop or the size closed, and why. Save the updated chart as In-trade management, tied to that action. The LAT asks for one chart per action.
  9. OUT
    At exit: History row check Screenshot History, Closed in the last 24 hours, then Order Evidence > Read > Send to Trades. The app finds the matching open trade by market, opening price and size. Choose the exit code (S1, S2+, ME, T) and whether you followed the plan, then Save.
  10. 5
    Complete the sheet you 5. Exports > Individual Trade Sheet Pictures: entry charts with their timeframe (4 Hour, 1 Hour, 15 Min), exit and post-exit charts, then Save picture(s) to this trade. Complete this sheet: your six boxes are already there from the decision; add the reason for exit and lessons. Then Save my text to this trade.
  11. DONE
    Gaps panel green app Each save above rebuilt the sheet in Dropbox. A chart slot you left empty gets a chart drawn from Yahoo data, labelled as such. Replace it with your IG chart when you can.
    Final Word + PDF in Individual Trade Sheets; Trade_Blotter.xlsx updated in Jose-F_Zubizarreta
If a limit or stop order never fillsDecision Panel > 2. Decision journal: select the order, Check the order against prices, then Mark expired and save the 'order not filled' Trade Sheet. It saves Individual_Trade_Sheet_Brent_<date>_order_not_filled .docx + .pdf, with your words and a chart. Add an IG History screenshot if you have one.

Today

Daily checklist and fixes

Today's run

Ticks are kept in this browser for today only.

Decision Panel says "NOT aligned to IG"

No IG price yet. Read the ticket screenshot or type the IG Mid price in IG price now. Without it, Yahoo levels can be 150 to 250 points away from IG.

The trade did not link to my decision

It links only when market, side and day match, the decision was saved before the entry, and the entries are within 0.5%. Otherwise use Decision journal: select the decision, choose the trade under It filled: link it to this trade, then Link.

Entry time is empty

The Positions screen has no clock. Take History > Activity, read it in Order Evidence and send it: the app opens the trade in Edit trade with the real time. Press Save.

Dropbox shows ✗ or nothing was saved

Check that Dropbox is installed at C:\Users\jfz00\Dropbox and running. Setup > Test Dropbox tells you which part failed. On a server, the Dropbox app needs Full Dropbox access and a refresh token.

Word file saved but no PDF

LibreOffice is missing on the computer running the app. Install it and save again (any save rebuilds both).

Low / High still empty

Yahoo was not reachable or the symbol had no bars. The Morning tab lists each problem under the Yahoo box. Try again, or read the range from an IG hourly chart and type it.

A chart says the Yahoo prices do not reach the IG exit

Yahoo's Brent future and IG's price differ that day. Replace the drawn chart with your own IG screenshot in Exports > pictures.

Wrong trade closed by a History row

Choose the right trade in 3. Trades > B before saving. Then use Edit trade on any trade, open or closed, to correct it. Every change is a new version.

The Unit 3 rule the app keepsClaude only copies what your screenshots show and finds published data with its sources. It never writes your set-up, trigger, execution, reasons, lessons, plan, evaluation or SWOT. Those must be your own words and judgement (Learner Statement).
